De La Salle University RVR College of Business Decision Science & Innovation Term 3 · AY 2025–26

BIGDATA Course

The curriculum is designed around the Business Impact of data ecosystems and their strategic governance. Students will analyze the 5 Vs of Big Data not merely as technical attributes, but as critical variables that define competitive advantage or systemic risk within the boardroom. By evaluating the life cycle of data architecture through a managerial lens, learners develop essential frameworks to translate technical constraints into financial and strategic value.
